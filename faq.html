FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where do you ship?

We currently ship throughout the UK and Ireland, with international shipping available to selected destinations upon request.

How is the whiskey made?

JIWhiskey is produced and distilled in Ireland using carefully selected Irish whiskey. Our whiskey is matured and finished to create a smooth, rich character, with cask selection playing an important part in the final flavour profile.

What's your returns policy?

We have a straightforward returns policy. If an order arrives damaged, incorrect, or there is an issue with the product, customers should contact us promptly so we can arrange the appropriate replacement or resolution, subject to our terms and conditions.

Do you offer trade or bulk orders?

Yes, we welcome trade, wholesale and bulk enquiries from bars, restaurants, retailers, distributors and other businesses. MOQs and preferential trade pricing can be discussed depending on order quantity and requirements.

What's in the current range?

Our range includes JIWhiskey Classic, our signature smooth Irish whiskey, alongside our new JIWhiskey Orange, which brings a distinctive orange flavour to the range. Full specifications, including ABV, age statement and individual cask/finish details, can be supplied for each product upon request.
