Our story

Joyce's Country to your glass.

The Joyce name runs deep through Connacht. It's believed to trace back to a Breton name meaning "lord," carried to Ireland by the Normans and settled by Thomas de Jorse in County Galway in 1283. That corner of Galway and Mayo became known as Joyce's Country, and the family became one of the Tribes of Galway, the fourteen merchant families who ran the city's political and commercial life through the medieval period.

The Joyce name is also closely tied to the Irish Traveller community, where it remains one of the most recognised family names in the UK today. That community carries its own distinct culture and history, a nomadic way of life with customs and language that have held firm through generations.

Founded by Dougie Joyce in 2022, this whiskey is built on that same heritage. Not a brand invented from nothing, but a name that already meant resilience, tradition, and success, now poured into a bottle.

Every batch is made to premium standards. Smooth enough for a first taste, rich enough for a connoisseur to notice the difference. It represents heritage and ambition in equal measure, a statement as much as a drink.
